HOW TO PLAY IMPOSTER:
1. Enter player names (3–12 people)
2. Pass the phone around — each player secretly sees their word (or "???" if they're the Imposter)
3. Discuss: everyone gives one clue about the word
4. Vote: who's acting suspicious?
5. Reveal: did you catch the Imposter?
