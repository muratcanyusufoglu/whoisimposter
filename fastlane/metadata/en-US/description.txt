HOW TO PLAY IMPOSTER:
1. Enter player names (3–15 players)
2. Pass the phone — each player secretly sees their word (the Imposter sees "???")
3. Discuss: everyone gives one clue about the secret word
4. Vote: who's acting suspicious?
5. Reveal: did the group catch the spy — or did the imposter escape?
