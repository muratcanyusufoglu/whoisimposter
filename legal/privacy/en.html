Privacy Policy

Who's the Imposter?  ·  Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Who's the Imposter? ("the App") is developed and maintained by Muratcan Yusufoğlu ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and how we protect your privacy.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. The App is entirely offline. No account registration is required, and no internet connection is needed to play.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves certain preferences on your device only, using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage). This includes:

• Player names entered during a game session
• Game settings (theme, language, sound/haptics preferences)
• Onboarding completion status

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional "Pro" subscription. All purchase transactions are processed exclusively by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or process your payment information. Please refer to Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies for details on how they handle purchase data.

5. Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat solely to verify subscription status on-device. RevenueCat may process a device identifier for this purpose. We do not share any personally identifiable information with RevenueCat. For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not target children specifically and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since no personal data is collected by us, the App is safe for all age groups.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
